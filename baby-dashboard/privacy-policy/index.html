1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the core functionality of the Service.

1.1 Information You Provide

When you use BabyDashboard, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Baby profile information
    • Baby's name or nickname (not required to be a real name)
  • Care logs
    • Feeding session records (e.g., time, duration, amount)
    • Diaper logs (e.g., time, type)
  • Optional notes
    • Free-form text you choose to enter

Local-only mode (no synchronization enabled): If synchronization is not enabled, baby profile information and care logs are stored only on your device and are not uploaded for synchronization. If you separately use the optional voice assistant, the audio and limited context needed to answer your request may be processed online as described in Voice Assistant, even when synchronization is off.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate and improve the Service, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers used for reliability, diagnostics, or security

This information does not include baby profile information or care logs.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information only for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • To synchronize data across your devices (when synchronization is enabled)
  • To recognize and carry out voice requests when you choose to use the voice assistant
  • To improve app performance, stability, and usability
  • To respond to support inquiries
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use baby profile information or care logs for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Processing

BabyDashboard supports both local-only usage and optional server-based synchronization.

Local-only mode (no synchronization)

  • Baby profile information and care logs are stored on your device, without a synchronized server copy
  • Optional voice requests may send limited context for processing as described in Voice Assistant

Synchronization enabled

  • Baby profile information and care logs are stored on our servers
  • This enables backup, recovery, and access across multiple devices

When data is transmitted, it is protected using industry-standard encryption. Access to stored data is restricted and protected by appropriate technical and organizational safeguards.

4. Voice Assistant ("Hey Board")

The voice assistant is optional. We use the microphone and process voice information only to set up or provide the voice features you choose to use.

4.1 When the Microphone Is Used

Microphone access requires your device permission. The assistant listens only while the app is visible and voice listening is enabled, or while you explicitly record voice setup samples. It does not listen in the background. Turning off voice listening stops wake-phrase monitoring; outside an explicitly started voice activity, we do not use the microphone.

4.2 Local Wake-Phrase Detection

Detection of "Hey Board" happens on your device. While waiting for the wake phrase, short audio segments are processed temporarily in device memory. We do not upload that pre-activation audio to our servers or AI providers, or save a continuous audio history. Voice setup also happens locally: we retain numerical sound features for detection on this device, not the original recordings.

4.3 Processing Your Request

After activation, the app captures your request and any follow-up replies. We process information on your device where supported. In the current Android implementation, recorded requests are sent through our server for speech-to-text recognition, together with the app language and baby names to help recognize names accurately.

Once text is available, familiar requests can be interpreted locally. If local interpretation cannot handle a request, its transcript and relevant context, such as baby names, current care sessions, custom event types, and the current dialogue, are sent for cloud inference. Local intent processing reduces these inference requests; it does not currently eliminate the preceding audio upload for transcription.

Live captions may also use your device's speech-recognition service. Depending on that service and your device, it may process audio remotely under its own privacy terms.

4.4 AI Service Providers

Our server may forward request audio, speech-to-text transcripts, and the context needed for recognition or inference to an AI service provider. Services we may use include OpenAI, xAI (Grok), and TypeSafe AI. The provider and data sent depend on the feature and configuration; a request is not automatically sent to every provider. The current Android transcription and remote inference implementation uses OpenAI.

Provider information is available here:

  • OpenAI API data controls and retention
  • xAI API data handling and retention
  • TypeSafe AI privacy policy and data processing addendum

4.5 Recordings and Retained Information

We do not persist raw voice recordings in the app's storage or on our servers. Audio is held temporarily in memory for processing and released when the operation ends. We do not save it to recording files, databases, application logs, or backups.

This differs from information derived from speech. Wake-phrase sound features and learned command patterns may remain on your device. Care records created through voice, such as a feeding or diaper log, are saved like records entered by hand and follow your synchronization settings and the retention rules in this policy.

AI and device speech-service providers have their own processing and retention terms. Depending on the service, configuration, and legal obligations, they may temporarily retain submitted audio or transcripts. Our no-recording-storage practice is not a promise of zero retention by every third-party provider; see the provider information above.

4.6 Your Voice Controls

You can turn off voice listening in Settings or revoke microphone permission in your device settings. Voice setup features stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the app's local data. Turning off voice does not delete care records you already created. You can continue using the app without voice assistance.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA / GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA):

  • Local-only mode (no synchronization): Baby profile information and care logs are not uploaded for synchronization. Optional voice processing is described separately below.
  • Optional voice assistance: We rely on your consent when you choose to enable voice processing. You can withdraw that consent for future voice processing by turning off the assistant and stopping voice activities, or by revoking microphone permission.
  • Synchronization enabled:
    • Consent - when you enable synchronization and submit data
    • Contractual necessity - to provide synchronization and backup features
    • Legitimate interests - to maintain service reliability and security

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data.

We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With trusted service providers who process data on our behalf (e.g., hosting or infrastructure providers), under strict confidentiality obligations, including the recognition and inference providers described in Voice Assistant
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the Service

Baby profile information and care logs are never shared with advertising or analytics providers.

7. Children's Privacy

BabyDashboard is intended for use by parents, guardians, and caregivers.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children
  • Baby-related data is entered by adults for caregiving purposes
  • If you believe personal data has been collected inappropriately, please contact us

8. Data Retention

  • Data stored only on your device is retained under your control
  • Server-stored data (when synchronization is enabled) is retained only as long as necessary to provide the Service
  • You may delete baby profiles or logs at any time within the app

Deleted server data is removed from active systems within 30 days.

Raw voice recordings are not saved by the app or our servers. Device-only sound features, derived command patterns, resulting care records, and third-party processing are described in Recordings and Retained Information.

9. Your Rights

  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Request data portability

For users in the EEA, these rights are provided under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details below.
